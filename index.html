02 · Research

Research themes

My PhD research centres on orthogonal time frequency space modulation and the design and analysis of reliable signal-processing methods for time-varying channels.

R1

OTFS Modulation

Receiver design, performance analysis, channel estimation, waveform transformation, and implementation-oriented processing for rapidly time-varying wireless channels.

High mobilityDelay–DopplerDetection
R2

Integrated Sensing & Communications

Joint receiver methods that use shared wireless resources for data communication and target sensing in time-varying environments.

ISACSensingJoint receiver
R3

Signal Processing & Performance Analysis

Error-performance analysis, channel uncertainty, oversampled reception, noise processing, and low-complexity receiver algorithms.

PEPOversamplingEstimation
R4

Waveform & Hardware-Aware Design

PAPR reduction, SystemC modelling, FPGA-oriented transceiver development, SDR testing, and practical validation using MATLAB, Simulink, LabVIEW, and USRP platforms.

PAPRFPGASDR
PhD research topic

Orthogonal Time Frequency Space Modulation

AdvisorA/Prof. Yi Hong

CollaboratorsDr. Preety Priya · Dr. Viduranga Wijekoon
